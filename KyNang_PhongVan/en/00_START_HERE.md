# 🎤 Interview Skills (general, project-agnostic)

This folder is **not tied to a specific project** — it teaches you how to *present* yourself and your projects in an interview. Combine it with the per-project docs (agent-talk, cap-cuu-101, quan-quen-map, totp-manager, word-checker, outlook-mail-reader) for a complete prep set.

> 💡 The three most important things for a fresher/junior interview: (1) **pitch a project in 2 minutes**, (2) **explain a technical decision and why**, (3) **be honest about limitations**. All three are practiced here.

## Golden rules
- **Specific beats generic**: "reduced payload with a rolling summary" beats "optimized performance".
- **Use numbers**: "47 Playwright tests", "2,864 lines", "100–120 beats/min" — numbers make answers credible.
- **Be honest**: naming a limitation + how you'd fix it scores more than faking perfection.
- **Don't memorize**: understand the *why*, because the interviewer will keep asking "why".
